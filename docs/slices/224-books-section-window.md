# Slice 224 — Books: the section panel in a floating window

Status: **coded (2026-10-24)**, see "Verified" for what ran and what did not. Dependencies: slice 223 (the sections, their routes and the panel), slice 219 (the floating panel of the preview, whose drag, resize and memory of the place are taken again). Module: `Books`. Model for the ergonomics: the Media Helper panel (`editor-media-panel.js` of `evlist/wp-media-helper`: the link "Open in a floating window" at the top of the panel, the window moved by its title bar and resized by its corner, the same panel in the sidebar and in the window, a menu item of the editor).

## Goal

The panel of slice 223 works but is heavy when the post being edited is a **section**: the whole list of its contents, with three buttons on each line, fills the sidebar (screenshot of Eric, 2026-10-24). On a plain post, on the contrary, the panel is discreet and fine. Decided with Eric (2026-10-24): **the list moves into a floating window** designed like Media Helper's, and the sidebar of a section keeps a **summary only**.

## What it does

1. **The title is "Book section"** (panel of the sidebar, window, menu item), not "Section", which says too little. The word "section" stays in the texts inside the panel.
2. **The sidebar**:
   - on a **plain post** nothing changes: "In sections", the field "Add to a section" and the switch "Use this post as a section" (screenshot 2 of Eric);
   - on a **section** the contents are no longer listed there. The sidebar shows the switch, **"Contains (n)"** with the range of the dates (the oldest and the newest post), and, **at the top of the panel**, the link **"Open in a floating window"**. "In sections" stays, since a section can be part of a book.
3. **A menu item** in the "⋮" menu of the editor, as Media Helper has: "Open the Book section in a window" / "Close the Book section window". It is shown for a section only.
4. **The window** (plain DOM, `role="dialog"`, `aria-label` "Book section"): a title bar with the title of the section (moved by it), a ✕ button and Escape to close, a corner to resize it (at least 280 x 220 px), kept in view, **place and size remembered by the browser** (`localStorage`, key `booksSectionWindow`, in a `try`: without storage it is simply not remembered). On a narrow screen (a phone, under 600 px) it covers the screen. Open or closed, it is the same panel and the same state as the sidebar (one model, two views): what is done in one is seen in the other.
5. **The lines** of the window, one per post of the section, in the order of the section:

   ```
   ┌───────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ ▲ move up                      ＋ add before │
   │  Title of the post                        │
   │  2026-09-29 · publish · moved   🗑  👁  ✎ │
   │ ▼ move down                     ＋ add after │
   └───────────────────────────────────────────┘
   ```

   - **The featured image of the post as the background of the line, lightened**: an `<img>` behind the text (`position: absolute`, `object-fit: cover`, low `opacity` over a white line), **`loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"`**, `alt=""`; a post without featured image has a plain light line. The text stays dark and readable (the contrast of the title is checked on a dark photo). **The images come after the list** (see point 7): the lines, their arrows and their icons are usable at once, and each image is put in its line when it arrives, without drawing the list again.
   - **Top and bottom edges**: small icons with arrows to **move up** and **move down** (disabled at the ends), and icons **"add before"** and **"add after"**.
   - **Middle**: icons **remove** (with a confirmation in the line: "Remove from the section? Yes / No", Escape or No cancels, no dialog of the browser), **view** (the page; the preview link for a post that is not published) and **edit**, the last two in a new tab (`rel="noopener"`).
   - Icons are buttons with a `title` and an `aria-label`, reachable and visible with the keyboard (Dashicons, which the editor loads).
   - Below the list: **"Add posts…"** (at the end) and **"Reset the order"**, as in the sidebar of slice 223.
6. **"Add before" and "add after"** open the dialog of slice 223 (search, dates, statuses, selection) with a target. The chosen posts are put **just before or just after that line, whatever their date** (Eric: the dates are kept as they are and the order is manual; manual actions to sort, for example by date, could be added later). The new posts take the ranks of the place, **every post from there to the end gets a position** (the same principle as a move in slice 223: exact whatever the positions that already exist), a post that is in the section already is not moved ("already there"), and the rules of slice 223 are the same (cycles, capabilities, at most 200). "Add posts…" at the end of the list keeps its behaviour: the posts appear at their date among those that were not moved.
7. **Server**: the route `POST sections/<id>/contents` takes an optional **`before`** or **`after`** (the id of a post of the section; both is refused with `books_section_bad_place`; a post that is not in the section is refused with `books_section_not_in`). The **state** of a section gets, for each post, **`view`** (the permalink, or the preview link of a post that is not published), and, for the section, **`title`** and **`range`** (`from` and `to`, the dates of the oldest and the newest post, empty without posts). **The state has no image**: asking WordPress for the address of a size that does not exist yet can make the file (Media Helper makes the sizes on demand, in `image_downsize`, in the request that asks), so an image in the state would make the list wait for the files of every post (found on reading the code of Media Helper; the lazy loading of the browser only delays the download, not this). **A route of its own gives the images**: `GET sections/<id>/thumbs?ids[]=…` (at most 20 posts, only those of the section, answers `{ thumbs: { id: address } }`, an empty address for a post without featured image; the size `medium`; the posts, their metas and the attachments are loaded in a few queries, not one per post). The window asks for the images **ten at a time, one request after the other, once for each post**, as soon as the list is drawn; a request that fails is **not** an error of the panel (the line just has no image) and is not repeated. `Sections::add()` gets the same optional place.
8. **Texts**: the strings of the scripts use the text domain `otherguise` (slice 223 left it out).

## What it does not do

- No drag and drop (arrows only), no selection of several lines, no sorting actions (later, manual, as said above), no kinds of units.
- The window is for sections: a plain post has nothing to detach.
- The preview panel of slice 219 is not changed; its code for the window (move, resize, memory) is copied, not shared. A common frame could be extracted later.

## Code

On the PHP side: `Section\Sections` (`add( $section, $ids, $place )` with `before` or `after`, `place()` that gives a position to the new posts and to every post after them, `marked()`), `Section\SectionQueries` (what is read: `state()` with `title`, `range` and `view`, `thumbs()`, `search()`, `candidates()`; `Sections` delegates to it, the file would be over the 700 lines of the project's rules otherwise), `Section\PostStore` (`info( $id, $detailed )` with `view`, `thumbs( $ids )`) with `WordPressPostStore` (the permalink or the preview link; the featured images at the size `medium`, the posts then their attachments loaded in a few queries), `SectionException::BAD_PLACE` (`books_section_bad_place`, 400: both `before` and `after`), `Rest\SectionRoutes` (`before` and `after` on `POST sections/<id>/contents`), `Section\SectionEditor` (five scripts, the style depends on `dashicons`).

On the editor side (`editor/section/`, plain JavaScript, no build step): `section-model.js` (the state, the calls of the API, the error, the listeners, and the images asked a few at a time, shared by the two views), `section-dialog.js` (the dialog that adds posts, moved out of the sidebar view), `section-window.js` (the window and its lines), `section-ui.js` (the sidebar view, reduced to the sections of the post, the switch and the summary), `section.js` (the connection to the editor: the panel titled "Book section", the item of the "⋮" menu, the routes), `section.css`.

## Tests

- PHPUnit with a database (`BooksSectionDbTest`, 23 tests in all): adding before and after (first, middle, last; several posts; a post already there is not moved), a place with positions that exist already, the places that are refused, the state for the window (`title`, `view`, `range`; no image, and none asked for), the images of some posts (an empty address for a post without image, only the posts of the section, the limit of 20, the capabilities), the routes with `before`, `after` and `thumbs`. `BooksSectionEditorTest` (the five scripts and their dependencies, the style and the icons).
- jsdom (125 tests in all for the JavaScript of the project): `books-section-model.test.js` (10: also the images asked ten at a time, once, the listeners told, a failure that is silent), `books-section-ui.test.js` (9: the sidebar of a plain post and of a section, the link, the summary), `books-section-dialog.test.js` (6), `books-section-window.test.js` (23: opening, the lines, the images that arrive after the list without drawing it again, the images in batches, the arrows and the ends, add before and after, view and edit, remove with the question and Escape, reset, the error, the place and size remembered and kept in view, the move by the title bar, the storage that refuses, the size seen by the observer, a narrow screen) and `books-section-editor.test.js` (the connection, the menu item, the routes). A fake API is shared by the tests (`tests/js/section-helpers.js`).

## Verified

- **Automated**: 507 PHPUnit tests (with MariaDB 10.11.14) and 125 jsdom tests pass; `phpcs --standard=.vscode/phpcs.xml .` (the command of the CI) reports nothing. Two defects were found by the tests while writing them: the explanation of a refused section was cleared by the reading of the state that follows (the model), and the loading of an image through `picture.loading = …` is not reflected by jsdom (the attribute is now set with `setAttribute`, which works everywhere).
- **Real WordPress 7.1.3 (scratch site, 2026-10-24), the editor in Chromium (Playwright), logged in**: the sidebar of a section shows "Book section", the summary (`Contains (5) · 2026-05-16 → 2026-05-20`), the link at the top and no list; the window opens from the link and from the item of the "⋮" menu (and closes from the ✕, the link, the menu and Escape); one line per post, then **one request for the images** (`GET sections/311/thumbs&ids[0]=312&…`) after the list is drawn, the featured image behind the text of the post that has one (`loading="lazy"`), and a post added later asked for on its own; Down, Up and Reset; **add before** (the dialog says "before “Journée 2026-05-19”", a post found by its title is put before it whatever its date); remove with the question (No keeps it, Yes removes it); the links to view and edit; the window moved by its title bar, its place stored (`localStorage`) and found again after the page is loaded again; a plain day shows no link and no menu item; at 420 px the window covers the screen (420 x 800). The only error of the console is the 404 of `favicon.ico`.
- **Never tried**: **Firefox** (Eric's browser; the tests ran in Chromium and jsdom), the sites of the blog (testing, production), a phone for real, a section of a hundred posts (the images arrive batch after batch, and Media Helper makes the `medium` size on demand: to measure on testing), a user with limited capabilities in the editor, the keyboard use of the icons beyond the tests of the focus order (not tested), the look of the lightened background on a very dark or very busy photo (the contrast was judged on two photos of a scratch image only).
