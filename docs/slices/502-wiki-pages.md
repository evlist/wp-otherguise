# Slice 502 — Wiki: the pages (a content type, the space and the title)

Status: **mostly coded on the branch `wiki` (2026-11-06)**: the type, the space, the claim on save, `Pages::create()`, `wp otherguise wiki create`, the panel of the block editor, the classic box and the list are done; the filter by space, `wiki names` and the block `wiki/space-status` are not yet (see "Coded" below). Dependencies: slices 500 (the spaces), 501 (the names, as statements of `triples`), 233 (the menu), 209 (the default template). Context: [`../design/wiki.md`](../design/wiki.md).

## What it does

A **content type `wiki_page`** whose pages have a **space** and a **title that is a name** (slice 501), with the block editor, and the checks that keep the index true.

- **The type**: `wiki_page`, not hierarchical, `public`, **no rewrite rule of its own** (`rewrite` false: the address is slice 503), `show_in_rest` true (the block editor), `has_archive` false, supports `title`, `editor`, `excerpt`, `thumbnail`, `revisions`; capabilities of the type `page` (a filter `wiki_capability_type` for the reflection of `capabilities.md`); its **own top-level menu** "Wiki" with the lists and edit screens made as those of slice 234 (no quick edit, a column for the space, a filter by space, no box of the other plugins). Its `post_name` is technical (WordPress makes it; nobody reads it).
- **The space of a page** is a registered post meta `wiki_space` (shown in REST, protected), chosen in a panel of the editor and in a box on the classic screen; **required**, set when the page is created, **not editable afterwards** (moving a page to another space is the tool of slice 505: a move changes an address).
- **The name is claimed when the page is saved**: on `rest_pre_insert_wiki_page` (the block editor: an error 409 with the message "A page of this name exists in this space: <title>", which the editor shows) and on `wp_insert_post_data` (the classic screen: the page stays a draft and a notice says why); then `Names::claim()` in `wp_after_insert_post`. A **new title** on an existing page is a **rename**: the old name stays as an alias (its address will redirect, slice 503). A refused title (slice 501) is told the same way.
- **The default template**: a new page starts with the template of its space (`Spaces::find()['template']`, as `modes_default_templates`, slice 209).
- **The status of the information**: the body class `wiki-space-<name>` and the text of the space are given to the templates (a block `wiki/space-status`, to be written with the wiki theme; here only the data).
- **When a page is deleted** (not trashed) its names go with it: **the cleanup of Triples deletes its statements** (slice 104), nothing to write here; a page in the trash keeps its names (it can come back).
- **`uninstall`**: the pages are not deleted (they are the content); the statements of the module are (slice 501: the index can be rebuilt with `wp otherguise wiki names --rebuild`, the aliases are lost).
- **The service `Pages::create( $space, $title, $html, $status )`**: the rules and the claim of slice 501, the meta `wiki_space`, the template of the space; the **same service** serves the screens, the creation of the 404 (slice 504), the command below and, from outside the plugin, the script of the migration (slice 508).
- WP-CLI: `wp otherguise wiki create --space=<name> --title=<title> [--status=draft|publish] [--file=<html>]` (`Pages::create()`; `--file` gives the content as HTML, kept as it is: there is **no import and nothing that knows MediaWiki** in the plugin, see slice 508) and `wp otherguise wiki names [--space=<name>]` (the index, with `--check`: a page without a name, a name without a page, and `--rebuild`).

## Coded on 2026-11-06 (branch `wiki`)

- `Page\PostType`: the type `wiki_page` as above (no rewrite, no query variable, REST, supports `title`, `editor`, `excerpt`, `thumbnail`, `revisions` and `custom-fields`, the capabilities of the pages through `wiki_capability_type`, own menu "Wiki") and the meta `wiki_space` (REST, **sanitized to the registered form of the space, or to nothing**). `update_post_metadata` and `delete_post_metadata` refuse to change or remove a space that is set, so the space is **chosen once**.
- `Page\Pages` (the service) and `Page\PageStore` (the posts of WordPress, replaceable by a double): `create( $space, $title, $html, $status )` (checks the name **before** making the page, makes it with the space, claims the name, gives it the template of its space; if the claim fails the page is **deleted again**), `sync( $id )` (the claim from the title and the space of a saved page), `check( $id, $space, $title )`, `space_of()`.
- `Page\Saving` (the hooks): **before a save through REST** (`rest_pre_insert_wiki_page`): **409** `wiki_name_taken` with "A page of this name exists in this space: <title>", **400** for a title that the rules refuse (`wiki_name_*`), for a page **published, scheduled, private or pending with no space** (`wiki_no_space`) and for a **change of space** (`wiki_space_locked`); a draft may have no space yet. **After any save** (`rest_after_insert_wiki_page` and `wp_after_insert_post`, which call the same idempotent `sync`): the name is claimed, a new title is a rename (alias), and a page that cannot have its name goes **back to draft** with a notice on the next screen (a transient per user).
- `Cli\PagesCommand`: `wp otherguise wiki create --space=<name> --title=<title> [--status=draft|pending|private|publish] [--file=<html>]`, each refusal said with its code and the page that holds the name.
- Differences with the text above: **the title of the post is the title of the name** (a name that differed from the title shown would be renamed by the next save: a display title would be a feature of its own, a meta); the **statements are not deleted by the uninstall** (slice 501); the checks of the REST filter use the title and the space **sent**, or those stored when the request does not carry them.
- `Editor\SpacePanel` and `editor/space/space.js`: the panel **Space** of the block editor (a select of the registered spaces, with their label; disabled and explained once the page has its space). Plain JavaScript like the other panels of the plugin, tested in jsdom (`tests/js/wiki-space-panel.test.js`).
- `Page\SpaceBox`: the box of the classic screen (`__back_compat_meta_box`, so the block editor does not show it), with a nonce and the capability; the space is saved only when it is a registered one.
- `Page\Lists`: the columns **Space** and **Name** instead of the author and the comments, and no quick edit (it would rename the page without the checks).
- **Not done yet**: the **filter by space** of the list, `wiki names` (`--check`, `--rebuild`, the list), the block `wiki/space-status`, the body class of the space, the capabilities per space.

## Verification

- **Tried** on the scratch WordPress 7.1.x, with real REST requests from the logged-in browser (`wp/v2/wiki_page`): a page created in `biography` (the space in any case), the same name refused (409) and **the same title in another space accepted**, a page published with no space refused, a draft with no space accepted and given its space later, a rename (the old name becomes an alias), a change of space refused (400), a taken name on a rename refused (409), a title with a forbidden character refused (400). With `Pages::create()` and `wp_insert_post()` in a script: the template of the space is set, a refused creation leaves no post, a duplicate saved the classic way goes back to draft with its notice, a draft with no title keeps no name, and **deleting the pages leaves no statement**. The meta `footnotes` of the Footnotes block of WordPress **is registered for the type** (it needs `editor`, `custom-fields` and `revisions`).
- **The block editor itself, in Chromium on the scratch WordPress** (`wp-admin/post-new.php?post_type=wiki_page`): the panel **Space** lists the spaces; publishing without a space fails with the server's message "Choose the space of the page first." shown by the editor; after choosing the space the page is published, the select is **disabled**, the name is claimed (`Encyclopédie:Éditeur test`), no JavaScript error. The block **Footnotes** can be inserted (`canInsertBlockType` is true) and a footnote saved with its meta `footnotes` on a wiki page (done by script through the editor's data, **not by typing a note with the editor's button**). The list of the pages shows the columns **Space** and **Name**, the menu **Wiki**, and no quick edit.
- A page is **viewable** at `/?post_type=wiki_page&p=<id>` until slice 503 gives it its address.
- **Never tried**: the classic screen and its box (the block editor hides it; a classic editor plugin was not installed), a user who may not edit the page, the footnote typed with the editor's own button, `wp otherguise wiki create` run by the real WP-CLI (the class is tried with doubles; WP-CLI is not installed in the scratch), the Footnotes block on a wiki page, the template of the space on a real theme.
